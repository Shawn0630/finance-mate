    Mortgage & rates

    Canadian lenders: insured mortgages are usually capped at 25 years (30 for first-time buyers and buyers of newly built homes, since Dec 15, 2024); uninsured mortgages go up to 30 years. With no monthly payment entered, the app derives the payment from the outstanding principal, the rate plan and this remaining amortization.

    Rate segments

    Loading prime rate…
    Add one row per rate period. A month uses the latest segment that has started; months before the first segment use the first one. Rates are nominal annual rates with Canadian semi-annual compounding. Variable rates are projected at the value you enter — in reality the rate will change over time.

    The monthly payment amount (above, in this panel) is converted to a per-payment amount by frequency: regular schedules keep the same yearly total, while accelerated schedules pay half the monthly amount every two weeks (a quarter weekly) — 13 monthly payments a year. The payment day (1–31, leave empty to clear) is the day of the month your payment leaves your account; it only anchors the projection charts' dates for monthly and semi-monthly schedules, so the interest amounts do not depend on it.

    Value, outstanding principal & payments

    Custom months:
    The assessed values are plotted as one point per assessment year, through the last known year, connected by straight lines. The bars split each payment into its interest and principal portions (stacked, so a bar's full height is the payment); on spans over 10 years they are yearly totals instead of monthly ones. The current year is fetched from BC Assessment automatically (and records that year's point); earlier years are pulled from HouseSigma — once when the property is added, and again whenever you press "Pull history from HouseSigma". Pulled points never overwrite a point you entered by hand or one fetched from BC Assessment. You can still add, edit or delete points by hand.
